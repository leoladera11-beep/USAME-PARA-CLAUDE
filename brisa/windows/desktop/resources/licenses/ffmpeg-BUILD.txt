FFmpeg n8.1 unmodified source, LGPL-2.1-or-later. Static LAME 3.100 LGPL library.
No GPL or nonfree configure flags. No network. Local MP4/WebM remux copies original video/audio tracks.
MP3 mode decodes AAC/Opus/MP3 audio and encodes libmp3lame 320 kbps; this is lossy conversion.
Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n8.1 and https://lame.sourceforge.io/
Exact scripts and corresponding sources are included in the accompanying source ZIP.
